## KEY MESSAGING GUIDELINES

### Do's

✓ Focus on business outcomes (revenue, time saved, lead quality)
✓ Use specific numbers and percentages
✓ Explain how solutions solve real problems
✓ Be transparent about investment and timeline
✓ Acknowledge that results take time (especially SEO)
✓ Show client proof (testimonials, metrics)
✓ Use simple, direct language
✓ Be friendly and approachable
✓ Explain why something matters

### Don'ts

✗ Use hype words: game-changing, revolutionary, cutting-edge, disruptive, next-level
✗ Make guarantees about specific results (different for each business)
✗ Use jargon without explanation
✗ Make promises about timelines we can't keep
✗ Overcomplicate things
✗ Be pushy or salesy
✗ Compare competitors
✗ Use clickbait or sensationalism
