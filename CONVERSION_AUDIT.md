## HOMEPAGE AUDIT

### Section 1: Hero Section

**Current State:**
- Headline: "Websites that generate customers. AI that automates your business."
- Subheadline: Stop wasting time on manual tasks...
- 3 stats (150+ projects, $2.3M revenue, 98% retention)
- 2 CTAs (Book a Free Discovery Call, See Our Work)
- Trust indicators (No credit card, 15-min call, Custom roadmap)

**Audit Questions:**

| Question | Assessment | Score | Issue |
|----------|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Does this build trust?** | Stats are strong, but no third-party validation (reviews, certifications, logos) yet visible | 7/10 | Missing early trust signals (client logos still below) |
| **Does this explain value?** | "Generate customers" and "automate business" are clear but generic | 6/10 | Could be more specific to pain points |
| **Does this reduce objections?** | Trust indicators help (no credit card, 15 min) but no mention of risk reversal | 6/10 | Missing guarantee/risk reversal language |
| **Does this encourage action?** | 2 CTAs present, but "See Our Work" competes with primary CTA | 7/10 | Secondary CTA dilutes primary conversion goal |
| **Can this section be simplified?** | Structure is clean, maybe too much information | 7/10 | Consider if subheadline is necessary |
| **Could this section convert better?** | Yes, strong foundation but needs refinement | - | See recommendations |

**Recommendations:**
1. **Remove "See Our Work" button** — Too many CTAs. Consolidate to single primary action (Book Discovery Call)
2. **Add specificity** — Instead of "automates your business," say "Automates scheduling, emails, and lead follow-ups"
3. **Add risk reversal** — "...or your investment back" in trust indicators
4. **Simplify subheadline** — Current one is too long. Try: "Stop losing business to competitors. Start generating 40% more revenue."

## CONVERSION OPPORTUNITIES - PRIORITY LIST

### 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (Implement Immediately)

1. **Multiple conflicting CTAs**
   - Issue: Hero has 2 CTAs, Services section has 3, too many paths
   - Impact: Visitor confusion, lower conversion
   - Fix: Consolidate all to "Book Free Consultation" or "Schedule Discovery Call"
   - Est. Impact: +15-25% conversion improvement

2. **Missing CTA in key sections**
   - Issue: FAQ, Process, Testimonials, Services Hero have no CTAs
   - Impact: Missed conversion opportunities in engagement moments
   - Fix: Add subtle CTAs ("Ready to start?" or "Let's talk") after each section
   - Est. Impact: +10-20% conversion improvement

3. **Weak service page CTAs**
   - Issue: "Learn More" doesn't trigger conversion action
   - Fix: Change to "Book a Consultation" or "See Demo"
   - Est. Impact: +20-30% service page conversions

4. **Unclear AI automation value**
   - Issue: 6 services listed briefly without clear ROI
   - Impact: Visitor doesn't understand what they're buying
   - Fix: Add specific benefits and time savings to each
   - Est. Impact: +15% automation service interest

### 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY

5. **Too many CTAs competing**
   - Fix: Consolidate all to single "Book Consultation" action
   - Est. Impact: +10-15% overall conversions

6. **Missing investment clarity**
   - Fix: Add price ranges ($5K-$15K, $3K-$12K, etc.) on service pages
   - Est. Impact: +10% qualified leads (self-selection)

7. **FAQ could be more prominent**
   - Fix: Move FAQ higher (after Why Choose Us instead of before Final CTA)
   - Est. Impact: +5-10% objection reduction

8. **Case studies could highlight timeline**
   - Fix: Add "Delivered in 6 weeks" or "Results in 90 days"
   - Est. Impact: +5% timeline objection reduction

### 🟢 LOW PRIORITY (Nice to Have)

9. **Add video testimonials** (if available)
10. **Add real company logos** (if available)
11. **Add guarantee/risk reversal badge**
12. **Add "As featured in" publications**
