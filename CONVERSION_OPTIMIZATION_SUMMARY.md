6. **FAQ** (Objections addressed): "Get Your Questions Answered"
7. **Services Page Hero** (Browsing services): "Find the Right Service"
8. **Final CTA** (Last chance): "Book Your Discovery Call Now"
9. **Header** (Any page): "Start Project" button

**Result**: Every visitor segment has multiple clear conversion opportunities at optimal moments.
